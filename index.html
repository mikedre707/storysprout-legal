StorySprout

Kids write their own storybooks

A story-making app for ages 5–10. Your child co-writes a real 8-page storybook with a friendly AI helper, decorates every page with stickers, then reads it aloud — word by word.

✏️ Co-write together

Pick a hero, a place, and a spark — then shape the story page by page with fun choice points. Every book turns out different.

🎨 Sticker scenes

Decorate each page with over 100 colorful stickers and backgrounds to show what happens in the story.

🔊 Read-aloud

The app narrates each page and highlights every word as it's spoken — great for early readers.

📚 A real bookshelf

Finished books live on your child's shelf to re-read anytime, and grown-ups can export any book as a PDF to print or share.

No ads No accounts Made for kids Parental gate on purchases

Made for families

StorySprout has no ads, no sign-up, and no social features. Stories are kept only on your device. The first 3 books are free; StorySprout Pro unlocks unlimited stories, premium stickers, and PDF export — purchases sit safely behind a grown-ups-only gate.

Get StorySprout

Available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store, and coming to Google Play — search for "StorySprout: Kids Story Maker."
